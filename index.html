The lay of the land

Two sides of Lake Washington

Seattle

A city of distinct neighborhoods laid out across hills, ridges, and shoreline, wrapped by Puget Sound to the west and Lake Washington to the east. Downtown and the water are never far, and each neighborhood keeps its own main street, era of architecture, and pace.

  • Puget Sound & Elliott Bay to the west
  • Lake Washington to the east
  • Downtown, South Lake Union & the waterfront
  • the ship canal & its bridges linking north and south

The Eastside

The communities across Lake Washington, from the waterfront cities of Medina and Mercer Island through downtown Bellevue and Kirkland to the Sammamish plateau and Woodinville. Lot sizes tend to run larger, newer construction is more common, and the employment centers of Bellevue and Redmond anchor daily life.

  • Lake Washington & the floating bridges
  • Downtown Bellevue & Kirkland
  • Redmond & the tech corridor
  • the Sammamish plateau, Issaquah & Woodinville

Frequently asked

Seattle & Eastside orientation

What is considered the Eastside of Seattle?

The Eastside generally refers to the communities east of Lake Washington, including Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Mercer Island, Medina and the smaller Points cities, Sammamish, Issaquah, Newcastle, and Woodinville.

What is the difference between Seattle and the Eastside?

Seattle tends to have older, more varied housing and more walkable, higher-density neighborhoods. The Eastside tends toward newer construction, larger lots, and employment centered on downtown Bellevue and the Redmond tech corridor. Both offer extensive Lake Washington waterfront.

Which Seattle neighborhoods have waterfront homes?

Lake Washington waterfront is found in areas such as Laurelhurst, Windermere, Denny-Blaine, Madison Park, Madrona, Leschi, Mount Baker, and Seward Park. Puget Sound shoreline runs along Alki and parts of Magnolia and Ballard.

Where are Seattle’s major condominium neighborhoods?

Condominium inventory is deepest in Downtown, Belltown, Denny Triangle, and the Pike Place area, with additional buildings in neighborhoods like Capitol Hill, Ballard, and Alki.

Which Eastside communities offer waterfront property?

Lake Washington waterfront runs through Medina, Hunts Point, Yarrow Point, Mercer Island, West Bellevue, Meydenbauer Bay, and parts of Kirkland such as West of Market and Houghton.

What neighborhoods are close to Downtown Bellevue?

West Bellevue, Meydenbauer Bay, Enatai, Clyde Hill, Somerset, and Northwest Bellevue all sit near downtown Bellevue, along with the downtown residential towers themselves.

What areas are convenient to Kirkland or Redmond?

Kirkland neighborhoods include West of Market, Market, Norkirk, and Houghton. Areas convenient to Redmond include Education Hill, Bridle Trails, and the Sammamish plateau.

How should relocation buyers compare neighborhoods?

It helps to start from your primary employment location and commute, then layer on housing type, walkability, waterfront or view priorities, and architectural preference. The comparison tools in this guide let you filter neighborhoods by these characteristics.

Is Mercer Island considered part of Seattle or the Eastside?

Mercer Island is its own city in the middle of Lake Washington, connected by I-90. It is generally grouped with the Eastside, though it sits between Seattle and Bellevue and draws from both.
