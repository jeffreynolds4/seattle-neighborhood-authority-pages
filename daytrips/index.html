Before you go

Passes and seasonal roads

Most of these trips need a pass, and several sit behind a road that closes for winter. Sort it out the night before.

Passes

  • Discover Pass State parks and DNR land. $45 a year, or buy a day pass online or at the trailhead kiosk.
  • Northwest Forest Pass Forest Service trailheads like Colchuck, Artist Point and some Baker Lake trailheads. $30 a year or $5 a day. The America the Beautiful pass covers it.
  • National park entrance Mount Rainier and Olympic charge per vehicle. The America the Beautiful annual pass ($80 for US residents) covers both for a year.
  • Sno-Park permit Required November through April at plowed winter lots.
  • Makah Recreation Pass $20 per vehicle for Cape Flattery and Shi Shi Beach, good for the calendar year. Buy it in Neah Bay.
  • Ferries Reserve a car spot for the San Juan Islands. Most other routes are first come, first served.

Seasonal roads

  • Highway 20 over Washington Pass Closes roughly November through May. Diablo Lake stays reachable from the west side most winters, but the drive over to Winthrop does not.
  • Chinook and Cayuse passes Highways 410 and 123 close November through late May.
  • Mount Rainier: Sunrise road Open roughly early July to early October. Paradise stays open year-round with chains in the car.
  • Artist Point The last stretch of Highway 542 opens midsummer and closes with the first big snow, usually in October.
  • Colchuck access road Typically closed November to May.
  • Hurricane Ridge Daily access in summer. Winter access is limited and weather dependent, so call the park road line (360-565-3131) before you go.
  • Snoqualmie and Stevens passes Traction tires advised and chains sometimes required November through March. Check WSDOT before you leave.
