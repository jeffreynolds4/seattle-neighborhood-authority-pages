How this guide is built

Personal selections. Specific checks.

This first edition draws from Jeff’s original patio guide. Each published stop has a checked location and operator evidence of outdoor seating. It is a selection of those original recommendations, not a complete list of Seattle patios or a claim that Jeff recently visited every place.

Only explicit operator information earns a covered-and-heated label. A fire pit, umbrella, scenic window or old photograph is not enough. Other seating details remain clearly marked as unconfirmed.

Menus, weather arrangements, private events and seasonal access can change. Confirm outdoor availability, your exact seating request and accessibility needs directly. No live opening status, reservation availability, prices or review scores are shown here.

Before you choose

Which patios are covered and heated?

In this checked edition, Terra Plata, Spark Pizza and Brimmer & Heeltap explicitly describe both cover and heating. Brimmer’s garden is separate from its covered patio. Confirm your seating choice with the operator.

Can I reserve COMO’s patio?

The operator says patio tables are first come, first served and cannot be reserved. A dinner reservation does not secure outdoor seating.

Does a water view mean outdoor dining?

No. This guide requires evidence of actual outdoor seating. A restaurant with a scenic dining room is not automatically a patio recommendation.

Is this a Bellevue patio guide?

Not yet. The checked Eastside selections in this edition are in Kirkland and Redmond. Bellevue candidates will be added only after their outdoor seating and location details are verified.

How does this connect to Jeff’s real estate work?
