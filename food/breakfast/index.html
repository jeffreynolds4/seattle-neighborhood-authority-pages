Before you choose

Where should I start for breakfast in Seattle?

Jeff selected Sabine in Ballard, Hey Bagel in University Village and Macrina’s Aloha Café on Capitol Hill as the opening three. They are personal starting points, not an independently judged ranking.

What is the Bellevue recommendation?

Chace’s Pancake Corral on Bellevue Way SE is the Bellevue stop in this edition. Cedar + Elm is in Kenmore and Salish Lodge is in Snoqualmie; neither is labeled as Bellevue.

Are all these places open for breakfast every day?

No. Some are bakery stops, some serve breakfast on selected days, and others have weekend-only brunch. Read the service note on the individual card and confirm your date with the business.

Are these the same places as the coffee guide?

A few overlap, including Macrina, Saint Bread and Volunteer Park Cafe & Pantry. This guide adds breakfast-specific personal notes and service details, while the coffee guide focuses on the cup and the café experience.

How does this connect to Jeff’s real estate work?

Local routines can help you explore a neighborhood. They are not predictions about property values or housing recommendations. For condo expertise, start with Jeff’s specialist profile; for area research, use the neighborhood directory.
