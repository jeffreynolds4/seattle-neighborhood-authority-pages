A few useful distinctions

Does this guide include the Eastside?

Yes. This edition includes Café Juanita, COMO and DERU in Kirkland, and Spark Pizza in Redmond. They are labeled by their actual cities, not grouped under Bellevue.

Are these Jeff’s favorite dishes?

The restaurants are Jeff’s personal selections. Menu descriptions are sourced business information, not claims about his usual order. A personal order or firsthand note appears only when Jeff has supplied it.

Where is My Friend Derek’s?

The operator currently lists 2108 N 55th Street in Tangletown, Seattle. Check its official site for the current pizza and sandwich schedule before visiting.

How does this connect to real estate?

A restaurant can be a starting point for learning an area. It is not a prediction about property values or a recommendation to buy a home. For housing and condo research, visit Jeff’s Seattle condo specialist profile and the neighborhood directory.
